# My offer, on one page

Keep every answer to 1 or 2 lines. If it doesn't fit, the offer is too big.

## Name

A plain name that says what it is.

>

## One person

Who is this for? Describe one real kind of person, not "everyone".

>

## One problem

What's the problem they have right now, in the words they'd use?

>

## One result

What do they have when you're done that they didn't have before?

>

## What they get

List the pieces. 3 to 5 lines at most.

-
-
-

## How it's delivered

A call, a document, a recording, a done-for-you piece of work? How long does one take you?

>

## My hours per buyer

Write down how many hours one buyer takes you, start to finish.

>

## How many buyers fit in my 5 hours

Leave room for finding buyers. Write your own number.

>

## Price

You set this. Two questions to help: what's the result worth to the buyer, and what price would make your hours feel well spent?

>

## What it is not

One line on what this offer doesn't include. This keeps it small.

>

## Proof I have today

Real things only: work you've done, a thank-you message, a before and after. If you have none yet, write "none yet".

>

## The one sentence

"I help [one person] get [one result] in [how it's delivered] for [price]."

>
