# Skill finder

The goal: find a skill you already have that someone would pay for. The AI asks these one at a time.

## The questions

1. What's your job, in one sentence a 12-year-old would get?
2. What do people at work come to you for, even when it isn't your job?
3. What do friends and family ask you to help with?
4. What's something you do fast that takes other people a long time?
5. What have you fixed, set up or organized in the last year that someone thanked you for?
6. What did you have to learn the hard way that you could now teach in an hour?
7. What do you do outside work that you'd do for free? A hobby, a sport, a craft, a tool you know well.
8. Have you ever been paid for something outside your job, even once? What was it?
9. What kind of person do you enjoy helping? What kind wears you out?
10. What would you never want to do in your 5 hours, even for money?
