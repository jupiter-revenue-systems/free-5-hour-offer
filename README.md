# The 5-Hour Offer

**The promise:** one small offer you can run next to your job. Who it's for, what it costs, a page outline and a 5-hour weekly plan.

By Ekai Stone, Jupiter Revenue Systems.

## Who it's for

People with a job who want their own income and aren't quitting. Creators who want one clear thing to sell.

You don't need a new skill. You need help seeing the one you already have.

## What's inside

- `START_HERE.md`: instructions written to the AI. It tells Claude or ChatGPT how to interview you and what to build.
- `templates/skill-finder.md`: the interview that finds a skill people would pay you for.
- `templates/offer-one-pager.md`: your offer on one page. One person, one problem, one result, one price.
- `templates/page-outline.md`: the outline for a simple page that explains your offer.
- `templates/5-hour-week.md`: a weekly plan built around the 5 hours you really have.

## How to use it

1. Check your work contract or handbook for the rule on side work. If it isn't clear, ask HR first.
2. Download this repo as a zip and unzip it.
3. Open a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT. Free or paid both work.
4. Attach all the files and send: `Read START_HERE.md and follow it exactly. Interview me one question at a time.`
5. Answer the questions. Give it about 30 minutes.
6. Save the 4 finished files in one folder.

Can't attach files? Open `START_HERE.md`, copy all of it, and paste it into the chat. Then paste each template when the AI asks for it.

## What it doesn't do

- It builds the offer. It doesn't find the people who buy it.
- You get a page outline, not a finished page or a checkout.
- It can't tell you if your price is right. Only real buyers can.
- It isn't legal or tax advice.
